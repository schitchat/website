How it works

Open the app. Something's already on.

No feed to scroll, nobody to follow, nothing to set up. The schedule fills itself from live US listings, so tonight's rooms are waiting when you get there.

01

Pick what you're watching

Tonight's shows, the channel each one is on, and how many people are already in the room. Tap the one you've got on.

02

You get a name

butter-butt. worm-gremlin. snot-boots. Everyone gets one on the way in — no profile, no photo, no follower count to protect.

03

Say what you think

The room reacts in real time. Good lines glow green, bad takes glow red, and the room's favourite sticks to the top.

The clever bit

Everyone's at the same minute.

Rooms run on show time, not the clock. Every message is stamped with how far into the episode it was said — so if you join twenty minutes late, you see what the room said twenty minutes in, not the ending.

And because the schedule follows your own broadcast feed, the West Coast isn't reading the East Coast's reaction to something that hasn't happened yet.

Save your seat

Be in the room before it starts.

Tap a show that hasn't started and it goes on your home screen. Doors open fifteen minutes early with a countdown, and the room unlocks itself the second the show begins — so there's nothing to scroll past and nothing to spoil.

  • Nothing to sign up for. Open it and you're in.

House rules

A room full of strangers, run like one.

Schitchat is for 13 and over. Everybody is shown the rules before they say a word: don't spam the chat, and don't be a dick. Every message can be reported, anybody can be blocked, and slurs don't post at all.
